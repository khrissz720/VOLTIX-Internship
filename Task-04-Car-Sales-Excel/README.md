# VOLTIX Internship — Task 4: Car Sales Excel

## Description

This task consists of creating an Excel sales table for different car types and calculating their total sales.

## Car Types and Prices

The Excel file includes the following car types and prices:

* Toyota — 590,000 EGP
* Jeep — 750,000 EGP
* Volkswagen — 460,000 EGP
* BMW — 790,000 EGP
* Kia — 375,000 EGP
* Mazda — 348,000 EGP

## Calculations

The spreadsheet uses formulas to automate the calculations:

* **Car Price:** obtained automatically according to the selected car type.
* **Count:** entered manually.
* **Total Sales:** calculated by multiplying the car price by the count.

**Formula:**

`Total Sales = Car Price × Count`

The spreadsheet also calculates the total number of cars and the total sales.
